T2108 is the share of liquid NYSE names above their 40-day average (783 names). Nasdaq 40 is that share for liquid Nasdaq names (604 names). The other breadth counts use 1,400 names. A 4% day is a close up or down 4% or more. The 5-day and 10-day ratios divide those up days by the down days. The 13/34 balance is names with the 13-day EMA above the 34-day EMA, minus the names below. A month is 21 sessions and a quarter is 63. The 50% count uses 40 sessions. Washout is a T2108 close at 29 or below. Extended is a close at 70 or above. A bounce needs a 5-point lift off the washout low and either a 5-day ratio of 1.5 or a 500-name up day. The breadth thrust, fitted to S&P lows that held, fires when T2108 rises at least 15 points in 2 sessions, give or take 2, from a reading at or under 30, give or take 2. The 5-day ratio is at least 1.5, and that rise beats the 10-day average. Nasdaq 40 is the same share for liquid Nasdaq names, and it uses that same band. A 10-day average still under 40 that is rising toward T2108 is catching up, not an improvement. Posture is half T2108 and half the index-health checks. At the highs means the S&P 500 is within 5% of its 252-day high. Not an instruction to trade.
